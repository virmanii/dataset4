# Task

You are the Director of Infrastructure at a multi-site national coffee company looking to advance your department by investing in a new solution for your growing team. You need to create a product analysis to determine if a program can solve your department's exact needs. You are currently assessing a product from ServiceNow called Configuration Management Database (CMDB).

You have been provided the following source documents for utilization in your output:
1. "What_Is_CMDB.pdf" - Outlines CMDB capabilities to analyze for Task 3
2. "Director_Budget.pdf" - The personal budget of the Director of Infrastructure that is referenced in Task 1
3. "VP_Budget.pdf" - The personal budget of the Vice President that is referenced in Task 1

**Task 1:** Create a document titled "CMDB_Product_Analysis.docx" that analyzes and extracts the issues or desires from the company summary provided below:

The North End Coffee Company was established in February 2020. While business has steadily grown, it is apparent that the IT assets are scattered nationally and have no centralized location to see all assets in one place. Coffee sales in stores are up, especially drive-thru sales, but sales in stores are down, and we need to figure out why. We are hiring a CMDB administrator who will oversee the product and be the Subject Matter Expert (SME) on it. We have five candidates we are interviewing in the next three weeks. We recently had a budget issue with our assets and want to be able to see the director's budget in comparison to the Vice President's budget in one view. We have a defined Asset Management process and have begun creating a Configuration Items (CIs) process, which we anticipate will be highly utilized company-wide. It is necessary that we find a product with asset discovery to incorporate into our process. We utilize serverless technologies and want to find a product that adapts to that. About half of the leadership at North End Coffee Company is on board with this solution, and the other half is looking at the alternative product we are currently analyzing. It is very important to everyone that we are able to customize the product and create a long-term (minimum of 5 years) IT foundation for the organization.

**Task 2** (dependent on Task 1): Provide a distinct yes or no answer regarding whether the CMDB can currently solve each item with its capabilities.

**Task 3** (dependent on Task 1 & 2): After completing Task 2, add reasoning for why each item in Task 1 is or is not currently solvable by the CMDB based on your professional experience and the source files.

**Task 4:** Provide a CMDB workspace graphic from the source files to familiarize the readers of the document with what the product would visually look like.

This document will be reviewed by North End Coffee Company's senior leadership team, roughly half of whom already favor the CMDB solution and half of whom are still weighing an alternative product, so the analysis needs to read as an impartial, evidence-based case rather than a pitch. Leadership will use this document as the primary reference during their final vendor decision, so each issue or desire raised in the company summary should be traceable back to a specific capability (or gap) in the source materials, not just a general impression of the product.

The document will also be shared with the incoming CMDB administrator once hired, as part of their onboarding materials, so the reasoning behind each yes/no determination should be detailed enough for a new SME to understand the product's actual capabilities and limitations without needing to redo this research themselves.

The goal of this task is to give leadership a clear, well-substantiated basis for deciding whether the CMDB meets North End Coffee Company's infrastructure needs today, or whether the alternative product under consideration should be pursued instead.
